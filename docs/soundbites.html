The absurdity is entirely inside the sentence. A Supreme Court justice interrupts a labour-law argument to admit he does not know what a footman is. No American context needed — the joke is a powerful man confessing ignorance of a word the law he is interpreting still uses.

Pozadí
Argued 16 April 2007 on whether home health aides are covered by federal minimum-wage law. The exchange was written up the same week by Legal Times' blog under the headline 'The Footman Cometh' and became a standard example in the literature on Scalia as the Court's biggest laugh-getter.
